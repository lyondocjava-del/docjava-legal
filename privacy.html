Privacy Policy

DocJava Stock Ticker — effective November 18, 2026

DocJava, Inc. (“we”, “us”) publishes the DocJava Stock Ticker channel for Roku devices. This policy describes what information the channel handles.

Information We Collect

None. The channel does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information Stored on Your Device

The list of stock symbols you add is stored only in your Roku device's local registry storage, on your own device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when the channel is removed.

Third-Party Connections

To display quotes, the channel makes read-only requests to Yahoo Finance servers for market data. These requests contain only the stock symbol being quoted; no personal information about you or your device is transmitted.

Quotes are delayed approximately 15–20 minutes and are shown for informational purposes only.

Children

The channel collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
